# Code of Conduct

Prunex is a technical project. Keep discussions respectful, relevant, and constructive.

Expected behaviour:
- Be considerate when discussing different approaches or levels of experience.
- Critique code and ideas, not the people who wrote them.
- Give clear, factual feedback and be open to correction.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insults, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without permission.
