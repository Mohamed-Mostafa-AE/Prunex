ABOUT PRUNEX

Engineering meets
the art of chess.

Prunex is an open-source chess-engine project focused on understanding and improving the components behind strong play: move generation, search, evaluation, and performance.

The project evolves through implementation, testing, and transparent documentation. The browser workspace is one way to interact with chess positions while the underlying engine work continues.
