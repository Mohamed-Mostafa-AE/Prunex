ABOUT PRUNEX

Engineering meets
the art of chess.

Prunex 0.5.1 is an independently developed C++20 chess engine built from scratch. Its own implementation covers board representation, position handling, legal move generation, search, evaluation, and the UCI interface.

The interactive board on this page is a separate demonstration powered by Stockfish.js 19 Lite. That browser component is not the independent Prunex engine.

TESTING & BENCHMARKS

Show the work.
Report the results.

The independent engine's regression suite contains 44 tests. The current Linux verification passed under GCC Release, Clang Release, and GCC AddressSanitizer/UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer.

Historical 64-game matches at 100 ms per move finished 0–0–64 against both Stockfish 4 and Stockfish 6. These results are shown as recorded; no Elo rating is claimed.
