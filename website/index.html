THE PROJECT

Understand the position.
Build the engine.

Prunex 0.5.1 is an independently developed C++20 chess engine. Its own implementation covers board representation, position handling, legal move generation, search, evaluation, and the UCI interface.

The interactive board on this page is a separate demonstration powered by Stockfish.js 19 Lite. It is not the independent Prunex engine.

TESTING & VERIFICATION

Evidence over claims.

The Prunex 0.5.1 regression suite contains 44 tests. The reported Linux verification passed under GCC Release, Clang Release, and GCC AddressSanitizer/UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer.

Historical 64-game matches at 100 ms per move finished 0–0–64 against Stockfish 4 and Stockfish 6. These are recorded match results, not an Elo rating or evidence of competitive strength.
